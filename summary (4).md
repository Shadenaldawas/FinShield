# SAMA — Paraphrased Summary (NOT legal text — see sources_log.md for official links)

The Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) supervises banks, fintechs, and payment providers.
Relevant themes for a new financial product launch:

- **New Banking Products and Services Regulation** — institutions must run a
  formal internal review (risk officer + compliance officer sign-off) before
  launching a materially new product, assessing new risk exposure it creates.
- **Open Banking Framework / Policy** — digital and data-sharing financial
  products (e.g. account information or payment initiation services) fall under
  a dedicated open banking regime with technical and business-rule requirements.
- **Regulatory Sandbox** — new, not-yet-licensed digital solutions can be tested
  in a controlled environment before full market launch — directly relevant to
  "target market expansion" scenarios.
- **Basel III capital/liquidity standards** — SAMA applies internationally
  recognized capital adequacy and liquidity requirements to banks; the *exact*
  demo thresholds FinShield uses to score an institution are simplified and
  clearly labeled "Demo / Prototype Logic" — they are illustrative, not SAMA's
  actual supervisory pass/fail numbers.
- **AML/CTF obligations** — financial institutions must run customer due
  diligence, ongoing transaction monitoring, and suspicious-transaction
  reporting regardless of product type.
