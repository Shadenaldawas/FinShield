# Vision 2030 / FSDP — Paraphrased Summary (NOT legal text — see sources_log.md for official links)

The Financial Sector Development Program (FSDP) is one of Saudi Vision 2030's
executive programs. Its stated aims include growing a diversified, digital, and
stable financial sector — including expanding the fintech ecosystem, growing
non-bank financing/investment channels, and deepening capital markets. FSDP is
not itself a compliance requirement for an individual institution, but it is
useful context: a digital investment product launch generally *aligns* with
FSDP's stated direction, which can be surfaced as supportive context (not a
pass/fail regulatory check) in the assessment.
