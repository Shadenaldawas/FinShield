# CMA — Paraphrased Summary (NOT legal text — see sources_log.md for official links)

The Capital Market Authority regulates securities and investment activity in
Saudi Arabia, including the Tadawul exchange. Relevant themes for a "digital
investment product":

- Firms conducting **securities business** (dealing, managing, advising,
  arranging, custody, or operating funds/investment products) generally need
  CMA authorization for that specific activity before launch.
- **Market conduct rules** require fair treatment of investors and prohibit
  manipulative or deceptive practices in how an investment product is marketed.
- Full and accurate **disclosure** of product risk, fees, and issuer information
  is a recurring requirement across CMA's implementing regulations.
